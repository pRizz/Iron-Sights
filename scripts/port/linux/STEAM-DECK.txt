Iron Sights for Steam Deck / SteamOS

This ZIP contains the same i686 game as the Linux tarball and AppImage, with a
launcher for Steam Deck. It is not a native x86_64 build. A 32-bit ELF loader,
glibc 2.35 or newer and working 32-bit OpenGL drivers are required. A real
Steam Deck has not yet been used to validate this package.

1. In Desktop Mode, extract the ZIP to a writable folder under your home
   directory. Keep the entire Iron-Sights-steamdeck-i686 folder together.
2. Put your own stock GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM in its Data folder. The ROM is
   never included in the download.
3. In Konsole, enter the extracted folder and run: sh run.sh
4. To launch from Gaming Mode, run chmod +x run.sh IronSights once, then add
   run.sh as a Non-Steam Game. The launcher finds its own folder and bundled
   libraries, even when Steam starts it from another working directory.

Data, saves, profiles and midas.ini stay beside IronSights. Preserve them when
updating. See START-HERE.txt for settings, HD artwork, crash reporting and
verification. The Linux tarball and AppImage remain alternatives.
