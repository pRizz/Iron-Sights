Enhanced fonts for Project Midas ADS

GoldenEye/GoldFinger texture pack by Carnivorous, published by Graslu.
Source: https://github.com/Graslu/GEPDTexturePacks
Revision: ca06e063f5b985cf59b1ed7a01d03ba3cf86ec63
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (see LICENSE.txt).

The embedded font masks come from the community pack's Bank Gothic and Zurich
Bold replacement images, rather than enlarged copies of the original ROM
pixels. This port adapts their padding to native glyph cells and stores their
intensity masks with lossless run-length encoding. Rare's original glyph
pixels are read from the player's ROM and are not included in this addition.
Original character spacing, baselines, outlines and clipping remain in use.

Only community font masks are included; the pack's ammo icons, logos and
Perfect Dark assets are not included. sources.json records per-image hashes.
Regeneration: scripts/port/generate_enhanced_fonts.py
